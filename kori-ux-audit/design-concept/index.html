Kori design refresh

A full refresh of every screen in Kori, on desktop and on a phone. The palette is Neel and Gada: Bengal indigo (নীল) for the brand and marigold (গাঁদা) for highlights, with no green anywhere. Income is shown in indigo with a plus sign. Every amount, merchant and date below is example data.
